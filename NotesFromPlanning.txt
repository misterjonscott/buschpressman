(the story) Historical explanation of this camera's existance.
In 1942, Fink-Roselieve (F-R), a New York-based manufacturer known mostly for photography chemicals and darkroom equipment, introduced the FR Reporter. It was a compact, press-style folding camera (2¼ × 3¼ format).
The Sale: Shortly after its introduction, the Fink-Roselieve company sold the design, production rights, and tooling for the FR Reporter to Busch Precision Camera (a division of the Emil Busch Optical Company).
The Aftermath: Busch renamed the camera the Busch Press-man (often referred to as the Busch Pressman Model C). Busch went on to continue producing and marketing it successfully in the post-WWIl era.
Following the sale, Fink-Roselieve shifted its focus away from camera manufacturing to concentrate on its highly successful line of photographic darkroom chemicals (e.g., F-R film developer) and adjustable developing tanks.

Jim Chasse noted, in GHQ 19-3 3rd quarter 2014 (referenced from his 2005 article in Camera Shopper), owning an FR Reporter with serial number 15,123 compared to our model, which is stamped 2024.
